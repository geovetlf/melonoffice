# @melonoffice/conversations

The conversations domain ([ADR-0033](../../docs/adr/0033-conversations-foundation.md)): contacts, channel identities, conversations and messages, tenant-scoped, with no provider types. Server only.

- `model.ts`: the records' rules. Deterministic ids (identity, conversation, inbound and outbound message), `checkInbound`, `applyInbound` (new address → new contact, never merged by name), `applyStatus` (forward only, `failed` and `unknown` final), status transitions and tags. For a person's reply ([ADR-0034](../../docs/adr/0034-human-tool-invocation.md)): `newOutboundMessage` (a `queued` text, id from the sender's key), `settleOutbound` (`sent`, `failed` or `unknown`, once) and `applyOutbound` (the conversation's last outbound activity).
- `repository.ts`: the `ConversationRepository` port and a memory implementation. `receive` is idempotent on organization + channel + provider message id; `reserveOutbound` stores a reply once per key, and `settleOutbound` settles it once, with its audit event and, when sent, its provider reference.
- `service.ts`: `createConversationService()` for the human inbox (list with filters, read, assign to a member or department, status, tags, contacts) on a resolved `TenantContext`; changes need a person acting directly and are audited. CV-3 ([ADR-0035](../../docs/adr/0035-conversations-inbox.md)) adds `inbox()` (each conversation with its contact, search by name, phone or email, sort by activity, creation or priority), `detail()` (conversation, contact, identity and latest messages in one read, the future `conversation` context) and `changePriority()` (audited `conversation.priority_changed`). `createConversationIngress()` stores what a verified channel delivered; it never runs a model, a tool or a workflow.
- `customers.ts`: customers and leads ([ADR-0053](../../docs/adr/0053-customers-and-leads.md), C1) on the same contacts: `createCustomerService()` (list by stage, card with notes, register with exact phone/email dedup, update with a revision, notes; `contact.manage` and a person acting directly), `normalizePhone`/`normalizeEmail`, `duplicateOf`, `contactStageCounts` and `consentAllows`, the check any future bulk or automated send must pass.
- `control.ts`: human control of a conversation ([ADR-0039](../../docs/adr/0039-conversation-control.md), CV-6A): the autonomy levels (`manual`, `assisted`, `supervised`, `autonomous`; `manual` by default, a restriction only), who handles a conversation (`human` / `ai`, AI `off`, `active`, `paused` or `escalated`), the closed list of handoff reasons, and `checkAutoSend`, the check every automatic send must pass: AI handling allowed, the agent in charge, the conversation open, and the control epoch unchanged since the turn started. The service adds `settings()`, `changeAutonomy()`, `takeOver()` and `handBack()` (a person), and `escalate()` (the runtime only), each audited with its change.
