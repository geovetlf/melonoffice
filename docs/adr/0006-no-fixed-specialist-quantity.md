# ADR-0006: No fixed number of specialists

- Status: Accepted (D-12a, closed by Geovet on 2026-09-26)
- Date: 2026-09-26

## Decision

There is **no structural fixed number of specialists**, neither per department nor in total. The quantity depends on the company, its nature and needs, its active departments and configuration, the roles it requires and its plan limits.

- Any initial composition (for example the Emprendedor launch composition) is **product data/configuration**, never a structural rule.
- Plan limits on specialists are optional entitlement values; a company may set lower limits but never exceed its effective limits.
- Both Home modes render whatever number of specialists exists.
- Automatic recommendation of a composition is a reserved extension point and is **not implemented**.
